## Player outcome and bounded scope

Deliver **one polished case in one workplace domain**: a product manager navigating a disputed software launch. Deepen the existing Relay launch case and its four stakeholders rather than building another scenario. Retain five decision rounds as a working constraint. The player investigates conflicting accounts, builds an understanding of what is known, chooses which commitments to honor, and sees how promises and deferred work change the room later.

Working interpretation of **decryption**: uncovering context, incentives and contradictions in workplace evidence. This is an investigation mechanic, not a cryptographic puzzle or access to real confidential systems. The knowledge base contains authored fictional material, not an external research service. The in-game backlog represents accumulating obligations; it is separate from the Jira development backlog.

| Required area | Proposed release behavior and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| One coherent political case | Reuse Mara, Ishan, Leah and Theo. Each has a legitimate public responsibility, a private incentive, partial knowledge and a credible reason to disagree. At least two conflicts cannot be solved by simply pleasing everyone. Avoid turning one stakeholder into an obvious villain or assigning a universal best personality type. |
| Case evidence and knowledge base | A compact, browsable dossier holds an initial working cap of **10 authored artifacts** across messages, meeting notes, product/customer evidence and commitments. Each has an ID, source, time/context, access rule and reliability label. New information can qualify an earlier interpretation without silently rewriting the original record. Provide readable text equivalents; essential clues must not depend on decorative images. |
| Investigation / decryption | At least two consequential contradictions can be examined through linked artifacts or stakeholder questions. The player can record a supported interpretation or leave it unresolved. Reveal missing context through authored rules; do not force a single inference from incomplete evidence or leak a private motive early. |
| Accumulating backlog | Confirmed commitments create visible work items with an owner, origin, due round, current status and consequence rule. Choices in later rounds can resolve, defer or reprioritize them. At least two earlier obligations compete later, and at least one deferral has an observable cost. Initial scope uses authored choices, not a freeform project-management tool or an additional scheduling game. |
| Decisions and delayed consequences | Preserve prepared choices and bounded typed suggestions with explicit review/override. Each round has materially different actions. Trust, relationships, information access and backlog state follow reviewed rules; at least two choices have later consequences. Record a backlog consequence once, without charging again through a duplicate score rule. |
| Evidence-based ending | A final case review connects decisions, knowledge available at the time, unresolved commitments and later effects. Separate recorded facts, game rubric and reflection prompts. Keep the existing event citations and readable decision record; assess the player's decisions in this fictional case, not employability or personality. |
| Designed complete experience | A distinct opening, investigation workspace, stakeholder conversations, decisions, consequences and final case review share one original visual language. Visual and interaction polish are required for this revised release, rather than an optional extra scenario, sound pack or animation project. |

The initial art/content caps and five-round structure are reversible working defaults. Product and Development may refine them through independent review within this one-case scope. Changes that materially increase cost, scope, or the target need an explicit owner decision.

## Visual quality bar

The owner selected [jesperlandberg.com](https://jesperlandberg.com/) as an inspiration reference. The coordinator's earlier rendered inspection identified a black stage, perspective project cards and restrained typography; this Product planning pass additionally checked its public description of a visually rich, motion-driven design practice. The required direction is **comparable care in composition, depth, typography, transitions and finish**, interpreted for Stakewolf's reading and decision tasks. It is not a claim of equivalent quality already achieved, an award target, or permission to copy layouts, branding, assets or code.

Visual Design and UI/UX should propose two original art directions with a substantially stronger sense of staging and finish than the current candidate. Explore a dramatic case opening, spatially composed evidence cards, restrained editorial typography and transitions that reveal relationships between clues, people and consequences. The current dark/serif/lime treatment is a reusable implementation starting point, **not the default visual outcome or quality ceiling**. Select the palette, type system and composition from the new rendered concepts. Keep perspective and motion in presentation where useful; provide stable, readable document surfaces and a flat reduced-motion path for investigation.

Two **owner review checkpoints** make the quality bar concrete. First, show rendered concept alternatives, including the opening and one dense investigation screen, and obtain direction feedback before broad visual implementation. Second, show the integrated game's opening, investigation workspace, conversation, backlog/decision state and debrief at desktop and narrow widths before visual/content freeze. Record the owner's findings and their disposition; independent UIUX/QA reviewers still verify usability and accessibility. Neither checkpoint has happened for the revised design, and screenshots alone cannot establish motion or interaction quality.

Polish must survive keyboard use, actual 200% zoom and reduced motion. Information and selection cannot rely on hover, animation or color alone. Keep readable contrast and the existing 44-pixel control target. No scroll interception or decorative sequence may delay an essential action. Prefer code-native design and system fonts; any added asset/font needs provenance, a loading fallback and a reviewed size cost. Proposed performance budget: at most **1 MB initial transfer**, no required third-party runtime assets, and no animation delaying confirmation. Development validates this budget against the first implemented design and records measured loading/interaction behavior on a named device; it is a target, not a measurement.

## Exclusions, assumptions and delivery risks

- **Excluded:** multiple domains/cases, multiplayer/voice, accounts/payments, a general-purpose task tracker, external knowledge ingestion, real workplace data, unrestricted AI dialogue, new paid services, hiring scores and automatic cross-session persistence. Retain the explicit clean-start policy; saving is a separately assessed feature, not a hidden dependency of the in-attempt backlog.
- **Assumptions to confirm through review:** existing case/characters remain suitable; decryption means evidence interpretation; five rounds and roughly 10 artifacts support a focused playthrough; client-only authored logic remains sufficient. Designers may refine reversible details, but these assumptions are not proof of player satisfaction.
- **Scope/content risk:** polished political writing and contradiction logic take more review than adding screens. Lock one case early, maintain an artifact/claim graph, and keep the three reference histories reproducible.
- **Usage risk:** a percentage allowance does not predict output, and account work may compete for it. Keep a weekly forecast and protect QA capacity; optional reset credits are not guaranteed capacity or a reason to overcommit.
- **Verification risk:** previously missing browser capabilities can block acceptance late. Secure a human or worker with the needed exposed controls during the first milestone; no coordinator promise to manufacture unavailable capabilities.
- **Visual/performance risk:** ambitious motion or asset work can harm reading, narrow layouts and review capacity. Prototype one real screen early, enforce reduced-motion behavior and measured budgets, and drop optional flourishes before required clarity.
- **Delivery risk:** prior candidate reviews do not authorize every later merge or public hosting destination. Prepare concrete runnable artifacts while coordination resolves the actual approval/access path; do not treat routine packaging as blocked by an unrelated approval.

The unchanged [quality severity gates](quality-plan.md#defect-severity-and-release-rules) apply: no open S0/S1 or unresolved acceptance-blocking S2 defects; permitted S3 issues need evidence, an owner and a recorded disposition. The coordinator's independent review corrected the visual-reference description, required owner review of rendered design, preserved completed Jira work as historical, and clarified usage/reset accounting. No revised target, implementation, schedule or release acceptance is claimed approved here.
