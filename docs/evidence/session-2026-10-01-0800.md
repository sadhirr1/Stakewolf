## Result

- Ordinary activation of Stakewolf home during play or a result now opens the existing restart confirmation. Keep playing receives initial focus; cancelling returns focus to the activating control. Confirmed restart owns focus on the fresh briefing.
- Intro, play/result and debrief main regions are focusable skip-link targets.
- Invalid written decisions show persistent associated feedback and return focus to the exact draft. Correcting the text clears the error. Proposal review focuses the suggested choice, or the first unselected choice when there is no match; confirming without a choice displays a visible associated error.
- Recoverable action failures display feedback in the current connected page/dialog as well as the announcement. Mutating page tools use the same feedback; the state reader remains read-only. The text distinguishes unchanged recorded decisions from a failure after state replacement.

Review corrections included prioritizing heading/start focus over the fallback main, avoiding detached error containers, associating the proposal error with its radios, and narrowing the unchanged-state claim to recorded decisions. No blocking source finding remained after independent review. The [QA record](session-2026-10-01-0800-qa.md) preserves check times, hashes and the final verification result.
