# Sera portrait brief

Create one neutral portrait of Sera, the human scout in Stakewolf's forest council. Use the accepted Orin portrait as the reference for realism, lighting softness and framing. This is the next art test; further expressions and characters come after we check her face in the browser.

## Character direction

Sera knows the paths and does practical work. She contradicts the council leader's account of an instruction, but neither her appearance nor her job reveals her hidden allegiance. Aim for watchful intelligence and restrained frustration, not a sinister villain or a smiling guide. The following appearance is a proposed direction you can adapt: a woman around 35–45, medium brown skin, dark brown eyes, dark hair loosely tied back, a few loose strands, a lean face with natural asymmetry and visible outdoor wear. Give her a distinct identity from Orin.

Muted moss and charcoal clothing, a weathered short wool mantle, simple linen underlayer and a worn leather cross-body strap suit the existing forest. Avoid ornate armor, glowing symbols and exaggerated fantasy features. Keep the face, eyes and fabric convincing at close range.

## Copyable generation prompt

Create a cinematic, realistic waist-up character portrait of Sera, an experienced human forest scout, for the narrative game Stakewolf. Use the attached Orin portrait only as a reference for visual realism, material quality, light softness and portrait scale; create a different person.

Sera is a woman around 35–45 with medium brown skin, deep brown eyes and dark hair loosely tied back, with a few fine loose strands. Her face has believable asymmetry, subtle under-eye texture, natural pores and sun-weathered detail. Her expression is attentive, composed and slightly guarded, with a closed relaxed mouth. She looks directly toward the viewer, with her shoulders turned just a little toward the viewer's left. She must look like someone who knows the forest and has a story to defend; her expression must not establish whether she is honest or deceptive.

Dress her in a practical, faded moss-green linen layer, a charcoal wool mantle ending around the shoulders, and a worn brown leather cross-body strap. Show restrained wear, fine stitching and realistic fabric thickness. Keep her face unobstructed. Frame from the top of her head to just below the waist. Include both shoulders and the complete hair silhouette, with a small clear margin above and beside her head. Keep arms relaxed and hands below the frame. Place the face in the upper third of a vertical 3:4 composition.

Use a soft, broad, neutral-to-cool key light from upper camera-left, gentle fill, and only a faint warm reflected light from below. Preserve readable eyes and natural shadow detail. Match Orin's grounded cinematic realism. Keep focus on the eyes and face; retain useful garment detail. Render a clean isolated character on a genuinely transparent background with natural alpha edges around hair and clothing. Deliver a PNG with transparency, ideally 1792 by 2400 pixels or comparable resolution. No environment, floor, shadow plane, white backdrop, baked checkerboard, text, logos or border. No glowing eyes, fangs, wolf ears, theatrical villain expression, glamour retouching, plastic skin or heavy rim halo.

## Before sending the image

- Send the original PNG, rather than a screenshot or a JPEG conversion. A PNG extension alone does not guarantee transparency: an editor's background should show through around her.
- Check the face at full size, particularly pupils, eyelids, mouth corners and loose hair. Keep both shoulders and all hair inside the frame.
- Prefer one strong neutral version. If your generator cannot export real transparency, send its best clean source and say so; we can assess extraction separately instead of pretending white is transparent.

## How we will use it

First test Sera beside the existing forest lighting, then switch between her and Orin during their conflicting accounts. The character remains a 2D portrait staged against a 3D environment; gentle whole-image movement and camera framing do not create a facial rig or lip-sync. Browser acceptance should check face quality, edge halos, readable eyes, matching scale and the crop on a narrow screen before another art pass.

After Sera, proposed cast priorities are a healer/observer and a worker or witness with a different age and silhouette. Their specific story roles and portraits remain to be designed. Hidden allegiance stays separate from public occupation and appearance. Local model experiments remain deferred.
