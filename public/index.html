FIELD GUIDE

Listen closely.
Decide deliberately.

You are the product manager at Relay, a fictional team building an AI meeting assistant. Five rounds take you from launch planning to the boardroom.

  1. Read the room. Each round gives you two conversations. Choose who to hear from; different questions reveal different evidence.
  2. Make your call. Choose an approach or write a decision. A written decision is matched to an approach that you review and confirm.
  3. Watch the consequences. Read immediate reactions and later callbacks. Delivery, trust, and product quality move independently.
  4. Read the debrief. Review the evidence behind your outcome and discover the stakeholders’ private agendas.

This first edition uses authored dialogue and explicit game rules. It does not use a live AI model or measure your personality. Progress lasts for this page session; refreshing starts over.
