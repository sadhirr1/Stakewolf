## Retained content

Actual parsed body: 7,071 triangles, 21,213 expanded vertices, 41 skin joints, one opaque material. The source advertises 7,072 triangles; the smaller actual imported count is recorded here. Two authored clips are retained: `Idle` (4 seconds) and `IdlePant` (3 seconds), 126 tracks each. No walking or lip-sync system is represented as implemented by this file.

The separate saddle/bridle (`WargTack`) and mask (`WargMask`) are removed. The source's non-display vertex-color/part mask is removed, since glTF otherwise multiplies the mask into the coat. An initial actual browser inspection exposed this as purple tufts and a green jaw; that defect was fixed before integration.

An outer wrapper places the base at y=0 and normalizes height to 1.2 m without stretching axes or editing local rig rest transforms. Front is +Z. Bind bounds are x [-0.371288, 0.325443], y [0, 1.2], z [-0.944208, 1.204698]. `Head` is approximately [0, 1.015942, 0.801977]; `Neck1`, `Neck2` and `Jaw` are retained.

## Conversion and size

`scripts/asset-prep-warg.mjs` uses the project's Three.js 0.186.1 FBXLoader/GLTFExporter and separately supplied Sharp 0.35.5. It is an offline data conversion, with no downloads, installation or execution of creator scripts. Run it against the extracted `Creatures/Beasts` directory and an empty staging output directory, supplying the path of an available Sharp module as the third argument. An optional fourth argument `lossless` retains PNG textures and original animation keys for comparison. Only `warg.glb` is shipped; staging glTF, textures and reports are not runtime dependencies.

- Equivalent identity bone duplicates introduced by FBXLoader for multiple meshes are canonicalized only after their world bind matrices match within 1e-7. The body skin weights and inverse bind matrices are retained. Independent review compared every bind vertex to the unmodified source; maximum error was 5.50e-8 m under the intended outer wrapper.
- The original texture RGB and smoothness alpha have separate roles: the coat stays opaque; roughness is derived as 1-alpha. Texture rows are flipped for glTF sampling. Normal maps use the creator's OpenGL convention and ordinary glTF loading.
- All textures retain 1024×1024 resolution. Coat is JPEG quality 94 and normal is JPEG quality 98, both 4:4:4 chroma. Roughness remains exact PNG. The corrected uncompressed PNG candidate was 5,657,296 bytes; this version is approximately 44% smaller.
- Three's redundant-key optimization reduces `Idle` keys from 15,246 to 13,956 and `IdlePant` from 11,466 to 9,862. Conversion sampled every original key time and 120 samples/second; maximum interpolation difference was zero. No motion decimation or geometry simplification was applied.

The coordinator inspected the corrected PNG and optimized models in an actual browser under neutral and moon/fire lighting. Brown/gray coat and yellow eyes were visible; the source's pointed fur and coarse close-up detail remain limitations. These checks establish a usable animated candidate, not owner approval of final art quality.
