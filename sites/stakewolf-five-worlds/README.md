# Stakewolf — Five Other Worlds (SCRUM-36)

A separate, buildless design comparison and provisional portfolio for sadhir.tech. Existing Stakewolf prototypes are untouched. Story work remains paused. These are interactive visual studies, not complete game implementations or promises of finished 3D character quality.

Open locally with `node serve.mjs` at http://127.0.0.1:4183/.

## Directions

1. Afterlight: spatial exploration, light and absence. Most demanding environment/character production.
2. The Verdict: interactive theatre, competing accounts and a divided portrait. Strong direction with a smaller asset set.
3. Chorus: a community represented as a growing organism. The most radical departure; requires careful legibility of simulation effects.
4. Cabinet of Motives: a tactile collection of objects that reveal people. Recommended small production test for an unusual, polished portfolio experience.
5. Dead Air: a signal-hunting experience. Visual equivalent first; audio production would follow selection.

No paid services, images, fonts, plugins, analytics, external libraries or live AI calls. Original Canvas 2D scenes express the art directions; they are not finished 3D worlds. Motion can pause, respects reduced-motion preferences, pauses when hidden, and caps rendering at 30fps / 1.5 device pixel ratio. Native range/button controls support keyboard and touch. One favourite is saved in localStorage when available.
